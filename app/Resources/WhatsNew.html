What's New

0.2.0

  • A new menu bar app: one panel with Today and Month tabs, a title you can set per account, and seven themes.
  • Pace colours: green, amber or red tell you whether you are ahead of the clock, not just how full the meter is.
  • Status-line backup: when it is on, jusage takes its readings from Claude Code's own replies and calls Anthropic far less.
  • Check every 15 seconds to 10 minutes (default 60 seconds), in Settings.
  • Updates arrive by themselves: App menu › Check for Updates…, plus a quiet check each launch.
  • Quick Keys (⇧⌃K): every shortcut in one list, click one to change it.
  • Send Report… mails the log to the maker. Help › Run Doctor checks the app and the engine.
  • Uninstall jusage… puts everything in the Trash and puts Claude Code's status line back first.
